The club

Started by players.
Built for young athletes.

The Fruit Bats were set up by experienced American football players who wanted to teach young people the game and help them grow as athletes and as people.

We run U11, U14 and U17 squads, and every one of them welcomes girls and boys. You don’t need experience or equipment. You just need to be willing to work hard and learn.

We’ve won multiple national championships and have players representing Great Britain.

Play for fun or play for GB. There’s a place for you at the Bats.

The game

Flag football in sixty seconds

No tackling
Every player wears a belt with flags. You stop the ball carrier by pulling one off.
5-a-side
Only five on each team, so everyone gets the ball.
20 minutes
Matches are short and fast, so a tournament day packs in plenty of football.
Kit
Shorts, a training T-shirt and astro boots. No pads, no helmets.
