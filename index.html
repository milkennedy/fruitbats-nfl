Get Involved

Start your flag football journey with the Fruit Bats.

What's Involved?

When: Tuesday evenings and Saturday afternoons in Ealing, West London

Session Length: 60 or 90 minutes of coaching and competitive play

What You Need: Shorts, training T-shirt, and astro boots. That's it.

Why Flag Football?

Non-contact, inclusive, and incredibly fun. Tackle by tag removal, not impact. Perfect for developing skills, confidence, and teamwork.

No experience necessary. Come try a free taster session with no obligation.
